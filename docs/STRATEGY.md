# Afritrace winning strategy

## Positioning (not “EUDR software”)

**Afritrace is the AI-native evidence network for Africa-to-EU trade:** it turns incomplete supplier data into verified, reusable, buyer-ready compliance packs.

## Competitor gap we attack

Last mile: collecting, validating, correcting, and **reusing** evidence from African farmers, cooperatives, exporters, and mid-sized EU importers.

| Competitor type | Their strength | Our exploit |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------|
| Enterprise (osapiens, e2open) | Workflow, ERP | Too heavy for mid importers + African suppliers |
| Satellite (Orbify, LiveEO) | Deforestation maps | Don’t fix messy evidence loops |
| Traceability (Sourcemap, BanQu) | Chain maps | Producers often don’t own data |
| National systems (GCTS) | Legitimacy | Not commercial buyer workflow |
| Generic EUDR tools | DDS/TRACES forms | Don’t complete incomplete evidence |

## Three layers

1. **Supplier Evidence Wallet** — reusable plots, certs, harvest, photos (supplier owns data)
2. **AI Compliance Ops** — extract, validate, precise remediation requests
3. **EU Buyer Deal Room** — status, risk, map, pack, DDS-ready data (assistive)

## Killer workflow

WhatsApp/email/upload → extract → validate → exact gap request → supplier corrects once → wallet stores → buyer pack → next shipment reuses 70–90%

## North-star metric

**Verified evidence completion rate per shipment** (target: under 60% → over 90%)

## Day-1 offer

“Send us your next cocoa shipment’s supplier documents. We return an EUDR-ready evidence pack in 72 hours for €750.”

## Revenue

## 30-day validation

- Week 1: 25 EU buyer interviews
- Week 2: 15 African exporter/coop interviews
- Week 3: 5 **paid** pilots (€750+)
- Week 4: proceed only if 3+ paid shipments, 2+ repeat buyers, 1 reused wallet

## Corridor focus

Côte d’Ivoire or Ghana cocoa → Belgium / Netherlands / France.

## Guardrail

Complement national systems (GCTS, CI); do not replace them. Interoperability layer, not competitor to government.
