# Afritrace monetization

## Core principle
Sell **outcomes** (EU-ready files that keep contracts), not "AI".

## Revenue streams

1. **SaaS annual** (primary)
   - Pilot: €5,000 once (90 days, 1 module, credit to annual)
   - Growth: €15,000/year (CBAM or EUDR)
   - Scale: €35,000/year (2+ modules, multi-site)
   - Enterprise: custom (Battery + SSO + escrow)

2. **Verification**
   - €1,000–€5,000 per supplier per year
   - Buyers pay to see verified African suppliers

3. **Escrow take rate** (later)
   - 3% buyer + 3% supplier on milestone trades
   - Only after compliance docs exist

## Unit economics (target)

- Gross margin after AI/OCR APIs: **≥70%** on SaaS
- CAC: partner intros + LinkedIn outreach to exporters (ZA steel, GH cocoa, CD cobalt)
- 10 paid pilots in year 1 → €50k–€150k cash
- Convert 50%+ pilots to annual

## Sales motion

1. Free calculator on landing (liability shock)
2. Email pilot offer (€5k)
3. Deliver one real XML/PDF/DDS in 2 weeks
4. Convert to Growth/Scale before pilot ends
5. Expand modules when next regulation bites

## Why customers pay

- CBAM defaults cost more than accurate data
- EUDR non-compliance = blocked shipment / up to 4% turnover fine
- Battery passport mandatory Feb 2027
- Consultants: €50k–€500k vs our €5k–€50k

## Do not

- Give away full platform free
- Compete only on EU importer enterprise deals without African distribution
- Underprice below €5k (signals low value)
